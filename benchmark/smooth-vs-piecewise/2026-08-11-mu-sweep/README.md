# Smooth vs piecewise primitives: mu sweep

Comparison of the two CommonMath primitive families for a journal
paper/letter. The smooth family is the existing C-infinity tanh/softplus
set with sharpness mu; the piecewise family is the exact classical model:
ramp/qramp composed exactly from fmax, and the step gates evaluated as the
exact heaviside (`math.mode` in the study file selects the family at
runtime, `math.mu` sets the smooth scale; see
`application/PhasorDynamics/README.md` and `../event-mode-proposal.md` for
the gate design and its implementation notes).

## Arms

- **smooth**: the existing C-infinity primitives, swept over mu in
  [1e1, 1e4]. Below mu = 10 the approximation is too coarse to represent
  the model (trajectory error near 1e-1 on GENSAL) and initialization
  fails at scale (mu = 1 aborts SystemModel verification on WECC240 and
  ACTIVSg10k), so the study range starts at 10.
- **piecewise**: one exact model per case, parameter-free. ramp/qramp are
  fmax compositions (exact at any mu); gates switch discontinuously
  through `Math::heaviside`, re-evaluated from the current iterate on
  every residual and Jacobian call, with a structurally zero derivative
  (the frozen-branch Jacobian). Crossings are resolved by IDA's step
  control, not by event location; event location is the reserved
  escalation (`../event-mode-proposal.md`).

Each cell runs at the default tolerance (1e-5/1e-7) for the measured point
and at tight tolerance (1e-9/1e-11) as a reference: the tight piecewise
run is the exact-model reference for every smooth run of its case; the
tight same-mode run isolates pure integration error.

Every canonical case runs its machines unsaturated (S10 = S12 = 0), so the
qramp activity there is the IEEET1 exciter's saturation; the machine
saturation qramp (GENROU round-rotor and GENSAL salient forms) is exercised
only by the study-local `cases/` variants. The `*Sat` pair sets S10 = 0.1,
S12 = 0.4: `ThreeBusGenrouSat` (machine qramp only, no gates, twin of the
`ThreeBusBasic` control) and `TwoBusGensalSat` (machine qramp on top of the
exciter and governor). The `*Bnd` quadruple is the boundary-stress
experiment: every machine's saturation knee is placed at its computed
steady-state operating point (GENROU: SA = |psi''|_0 per machine; GENSAL:
SA = Eqp_0) and every governor's Pvmax is pinned at pmech0, so each
nonsmooth point sits exactly where the system operates. Placing Pvmax
found that the canonical TwoBus cases already operate at Pvmax = pmech0
= 1.0, which is why their gate traffic was heavy from the start.

## Captured per run

- `ida_stats.json`: per-segment and total steps, residual/Jacobian
  evaluations, linear solver setups, error-test failures, nonlinear
  iterations/failures.
- `ida_steps.json`: every accepted step's size, order, cj, per-step work
  deltas, and the WRMS local truncation error (`lte_wrms`, 1.0 = at the
  error-test limit).
- `stdout.txt`: `GRIDKIT_PROFILE` and `SYSTEM_RESIDUAL_PROFILE` timing
  blocks, system size, and the `math_mode`/`math_mu` provenance echo.
- `study_out.csv`: monitor trajectories on the shared `dt_monitor` grid,
  used for cross-run trajectory error.

## Results

662 runs, 660 completed; the only failures are the ACTIVSg10k piecewise
pair (finding 4). `matrix-run.log` is the raw run record across the five
campaigns (PWL-gate arms and mu < 10 probes from the first two remain in
the log; the current matrix supersedes them).

### Findings

1. **Smoothing error decays like 1/mu and the runtime knob closes the
   model gap.** The smooth trajectory's deviation from the exact model
   falls monotonically over the swept three decades (TwoBusGensal: 5.9e-2
   at mu = 10 to 7.4e-5 at mu = 1e4; TwoBusIeeet1: 7.8e-3 to 3.7e-5)
   while the exact arm sits at its integration-error floor (1.8e-6 to
   7.6e-6). At mu = 1e4 the smooth model is within roughly an order of
   magnitude of that floor. `figures/error_vs_mu.png`

2. **Exactness is free for qramp and expensive for the gates.**
   ThreeBusGenrouSat, whose only active primitive is the machine
   saturation qramp, runs its exact arm at literal counter parity with
   smooth (500 steps, 52 Jacobians, both arms, every counter equal in the
   heatmap) and the two arms' step sequences coincide: Newton and the BDF
   error estimator absorb the C1 kink outright. Every gate-bearing case
   pays: the exact arm needs 1.3-3.7x the Jacobian evaluations of smooth
   mu = 10^2 (WECC240 218 vs 58) and accumulates Newton failures where
   smooth has none (TGOV1 17 vs 0, IEEET1 26 vs 2), and the step-size
   traces show why: each gate crossing is localized by error-test
   rejection and h-collapse (`figures/qramp_focus.png`,
   `figures/step_profiles.png`, `figures/work_vs_mu.png`,
   `figures/counters_heatmap.png`). The smooth family's own cost rises
   toward the exact arm's as mu grows.

3. **The machine-qramp model gap closes much faster than the gates'.**
   Measured tight-against-tight (below the default-tolerance integration
   floor), ThreeBusGenrouSat's smoothing error falls from 2.4e-2 at
   mu = 10 to the tight-integration noise floor (~1e-8) by mu ~ 300 -
   consistent with qramp's 1/mu^2 knee-error scaling - while the
   gate-bearing GENSAL cases decay only like 1/mu, and the saturated and
   unsaturated GENSAL curves coincide: the machine saturation contributes
   nothing to their residual model gap, which belongs to the exciter
   qramp and the gates. `figures/qramp_focus.png`

4. **At 71k states the exact model is not integrable through a fault by
   step control alone; smoothing is what makes it feasible.** ACTIVSg10k:
   every smooth run completes 10 s (1211 steps at mu = 10 to 1728 at
   mu = 1e4); the exact model integrates cleanly to the fault (110 steps,
   zero failures), spends 396 steps and 134 Newton failures inside the
   0.1 s fault window, and aborts at t = 1.107 s with h at hmin - within
   a millisecond of where the retired PWL-gate arm died, so the collapse
   is set by the model's corner density, not by any finite gate band.
   WECC240 (4292 states) by contrast completes in exact mode in 0.28 s
   wall. Event location (stage 2 of the proposal) is the known remedy.
   `figures/activsg10k_step_collapse.png`

5. **The usable mu range is bounded below by initialization at scale.**
   Every case initializes and completes at every swept mu >= 10. Below
   the range, mu = 1 fails SystemModel verification on WECC240 and
   ACTIVSg10k (REPCA PI limits, saturation seeds) while exact-mode
   initialization, which is sharpness-free, succeeds; this boundary is
   one reason the study floor is mu = 10.

6. **Control case.** ThreeBusBasic (unsaturated GENROU, no active
   primitives) produces identical counters in both arms at all 25 swept
   mu, confirming the families differ only where limiter/saturation
   primitives are active.

7. **Boundary stress: placing every nonsmooth point at the operating
   point separates the families further, and exposes a smoothing failure
   mode that counters cannot see.** With the knee at the operating point
   and no gates (ThreeBusGenrouBnd), the arms still run in near lockstep
   (513 steps / 57 Jacobians both) and the tight-vs-tight gap still
   vanishes quadratically (4e-2 at mu = 10 to ~1e-7 by mu ~ 700): sitting
   on the qramp kink costs nothing. The gate cases keep their gate costs
   (TGOV1Bnd exact arm 131 vs 118 Jacobians for the base case). The
   GENSAL boundary case turns physically marginal - with Pvmax pinned at
   pmech0 the unit cannot re-accelerate, decelerating through the whole
   30 s horizon - and both arms grind equally (6655 vs 6307 steps at
   mu = 1e4; ratios near parity), so the cost there is the dynamics, not
   the representation. But below mu ~ 300 the smooth model settles onto a
   different post-fault equilibrium entirely - Eqp locks to the knee at
   1.007 while the exact model rises to 1.055 - because the smoothing
   tail re-shapes the saturation curve exactly where the system operates:
   shallow smoothing at boundary operation biases equilibria, not just
   transients. `figures/boundary_focus.png`,
   `figures/boundary_profiles.png`

Caveat: the ACTIVSg200/500 trajectory errors (1e-2 to 1e0) are dominated
by phase drift of sustained inter-area oscillations over the 10 s horizon,
WECC240's mu <= 100 trajectory errors by a diverging post-fault controller
trajectory, and TwoBusGensalBnd's by phase drift of its marginal
electromechanical oscillation (except for the genuine equilibrium shift of
finding 7); the pointwise WRMS metric overstates model disagreement
there. The per-mode `ref_self` columns carry the same
signature, so mode comparisons on those cases should lean on the work
counters rather than raw trajectory error.

## Provenance

Branch `lukel/smooth-paper-dev`. One binary per campaign; mode and mu are
runtime study-file settings, so no per-point rebuilds. The exact-gate

in `matrix-run.log`); the smooth runs are unchanged by that commit and
carry over from the second campaign.
